1. Introduction

Godown Stock Manager is an inventory and warehouse management application designed to help businesses manage multiple godowns, cartons, stock records, and inventory efficiently.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how the application accesses, stores, uses, and protects information when you use Godown Stock Manager.

By using the application, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Stored by the Application

Godown Stock Manager uses information provided by users to deliver its inventory management features.

A. Godown and Inventory Information

Users may add and maintain information such as:

  • Godown names and details.
  • Carton names and identification information.
  • Product and inventory details.
  • Stock quantities and related records.

This information is stored locally on the user's device using the application's Room database.

B. Photo Search Information

Godown Stock Manager offers a photo-based carton search feature that allows users to search for cartons using images.

  • Users may select an image for photo-based search.
  • Image processing and matching are performed locally on the device.
  • Selected images are not uploaded to our servers or external image-processing services.
  • Images are used for the requested search functionality.

The application does not upload users' selected photographs to a developer-operated server.

C. Subscription and Trial Information

Godown Stock Manager offers premium functionality through a subscription.

The application may maintain local information related to trial eligibility, trial duration, and premium access status.

Subscription purchases and payment transactions are handled through Google Play Billing.

We do not directly collect or store users' credit card details, UPI PINs, banking credentials, or other payment authentication details.

3. How We Use Information

Information handled by the application is used to provide its intended functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing godowns.
  • Adding, updating, and viewing carton records.
  • Managing inventory and stock quantities.
  • Searching carton names across godowns.
  • Performing photo-based carton searches.
  • Managing trial access and premium features.
  • Checking subscription status through Google Play Billing.

We do not use users' inventory records or selected photographs for advertising or unrelated purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Godown Stock Manager is designed to store inventory information locally on the user's device.

The application uses a local Room database for inventory and godown records. Certain application preferences, including trial and premium access information, may also be stored locally.

Photo-based search is processed on the user's device. Selected photographs are not transmitted to our servers for image matching.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and protecting important inventory information.

Important: Uninstalling the application, clearing its data, or losing access to the device may result in the loss of locally stored inventory information. Users should maintain appropriate backups of important records.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' inventory information or selected photographs.

We do not share selected photographs with external image-processing services.

Google Play processes subscription-related transactions and information according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, regulation, or a valid legal process.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Inventory information remains stored locally on the user's device until it is deleted by the user or the application data is removed.

The application does not require inventory records or photo-search images to be uploaded to a developer-operated cloud database.

Users can manage their inventory records within the application. Removing application data or uninstalling the application may permanently remove locally stored records.

Subscription transaction records are handled and retained by Google Play according to its applicable policies.

7. Application Permissions

Godown Stock Manager may request access to device features when required to provide its functionality.

Photos and Image Selection: Access is used when a user selects an image for photo-based carton search.

Images selected for this feature are processed locally on the device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Users can manage application permissions through their Android device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Godown Stock Manager is a business and inventory management application and is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
